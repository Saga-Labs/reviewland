Grok Imagine alternatives for iPhone

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

Grok Imagine lives inside the Grok app (SuperGrok from $10 a month) and, on xAI's API, makes up to 15 seconds at up to 1080p with lip-synced speech. For 4K, or a clip extended up to 3 minutes, Kling AI. For video you edit by chatting, if you pay for Google AI, Gemini. To keep Grok as one option and add Seedance 2 Mini and Kling from the same coins, Dora.

All six are free to download and sell subscriptions or credits; none of these listings promises free video. Sound depends on the model, so check it before you generate.

1

Kling AI: AI Image&Video Maker

Best for: 4K clips, and extending a clip up to 3 minutes.

  • Its listing says text and images become video, up to 15 seconds in native 1080p or 4K, and Video Extension takes a clip up to 3 minutes.
  • Kling's website presents Kling 4.0 as making picture and sound together.

Limits: the listing does not say how many credits a video costs or mention sound.

Google Gemini

Best for: a chat app like Grok, with video you edit by asking, if you pay for Google AI.

  • Its listing says Gemini Omni makes video for Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra users; Google's site lists 10-second clips with native audio and editing in the chat.
  • The only privacy label here without Data Used to Track You.

Limits: video needs a paid plan, and Google's site says it is for users 18 and over.

Runway: AI Image & Video

Best for: choosing among several companies' video models and planning multi-shot videos.

  • Its listing names Seedance 2.5, Kling 3 Pro and Gen-4.5 among its video models.
  • Runway Agent turns a plain description into several shots and assembles them.

Limits: English only, no iPad version, and the listing gives no clip length or sound.

DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: keeping Grok as one option and trying other models from the same coins.

  • Dora's models page lists Grok (480p, 6 seconds, 10 credits) next to Seedance 2 Mini (8 seconds with sound for 31 credits) and Kling 2.6.
  • The coin cost shows before you generate and failed generations give the coins back, per its listing.
  • Templates put you in another era or place, and Motion Control copies a dance onto your photo.

Limits: a subscription with no free video, and short clips only.

Who should use what

  • 4K and longer edits: Kling AI.
  • Editing video by chat: Gemini.
  • Several models and multi-shot videos: Runway.
  • Grok plus other models from one balance: Dora.
  • The most models: Pollo AI.
  • Selfie fun: Hula AI.

Use cases

  • A 4K product shot: Kling AI.
  • A talking clip with sound: Gemini, or Seedance 2 Mini in Dora.
  • The same prompt on several models: Pollo AI, Runway or Dora.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data, xAI's API documentation for Grok Imagine, and Google's and Kling's sites for sound. We generated nothing and did not compare quality.

Order: first the apps whose listing says most plainly what video they make, then the many-model apps and the selfie app. For the models side by side, see Seedance 2 Mini vs Kling vs Veo vs Grok Imagine; for sound, AI video apps with sound.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Grok Imagine?

For quality settings, Kling AI (up to 4K). Inside a chat app, Gemini with a Google AI plan. To keep Grok and add other models, Dora or Pollo AI.

Is Grok Imagine free?

The Grok app sells SuperGrok plans from $10 and extra credits; its listing does not say how many free videos you get. On xAI's API, Grok Imagine Video 1.5 costs $0.08 to $0.25 per second, and the Lite version from $0.02.

Which alternatives make video with sound?

Gemini (Gemini Omni), Kling 4.0 per Kling's site, Dora on the models that support it, and some models in Pollo AI.

Can I still use Grok's video model in another app?

Yes. Dora's models page lists Grok at 480p and 6 seconds for 10 credits.
