Photo vault apps with a backup, so you don't lose them on a new iPhone

Updated 9 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 4 November 2026

A photo vault keeps its photos inside the app, so if the app has no backup, deleting it or losing the phone can lose them. Before you switch iPhones, check where your vault backs up. For an automatic backup in the company's cloud, Private Photo Vault (Cloud Vault, paid) or Keepsafe (Private Cloud). For Dropbox or Google Drive, PV or Calculator#. For a backup in your own iCloud that only you can open, Photo Vault or Converter.

In almost all of them the backup is a paid feature. Turn it on, or export the vault, before you reset or trade in the old phone.

1

Private Photo Vault - Pic Safe

Best for: an automatic, encrypted backup from the best-known vault.

  • Its listing says Cloud Vault is an encrypted backup with a separate Cloud Vault password, and that its staff cannot view your photos.
  • Space Saver keeps originals in the cloud; rated 4.8 stars.

Limits: its listing says the free version does not back up to Cloud Vault, so on the free version a lost phone means lost photos.

Secret Photo Vault: Keepsafe

Best for: the same vault on several devices, synced automatically.

  • Its listing says Private Cloud stores up to 10,000 items and syncs to all your Keepsafe installations on any platform.
  • Trash recovery for photos deleted by mistake.

Limits: the cloud is Keepsafe's, and the 10,000-item limit is in the listing.

PV - Secret Photo Album

Best for: choosing between its own cloud and Dropbox or Google Drive.

  • Its listing offers cloud sync for Super members and third-party cloud backup for Pro members, plus Wi-Fi transfer to a computer.
  • Its listing warns that if you delete the app without a backup, the photos are gone.

Limits: every backup option is paid, and the plans are many; its privacy label is the widest here.

Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video

Best for: a backup only you can open, in your own iCloud, with no account.

  • Its listing says the optional iCloud copy is end-to-end encrypted and lives in your own iCloud; to move to a new iPhone you turn it on and restore.
  • Or export the whole vault to one password-locked file in Files, on a computer or in iCloud Drive.
  • The vault is left out of regular iCloud backups, per its listing; no account and no server.

Limits: the iCloud copy needs Premium, and without it or an exported file there is no backup.

Converter: Hidden Photo Vault

Best for: your own iCloud with no subscription.

  • Its listing says files are encrypted and kept on the phone and in your own iCloud, opened only with your password.

Limits: English only, needs iOS 18.2, and its last update was in December 2025.

Calculator# Hide Photos Videos

Who should use what

  • Automatic backup, best-known app: Private Photo Vault.
  • The same vault on several devices: Keepsafe.
  • Dropbox or Google Drive: PV or Calculator#.
  • Your own iCloud, no account, only you can open it: Photo Vault.
  • Your own iCloud, paid once: Converter.

Use cases

  • You are getting a new iPhone next week: turn on the backup or export the vault on the old phone first, then restore on the new one.
  • You don't want your private photos on a company's server: Photo Vault or Converter keep the copy in your own iCloud.
  • You only need photos out of your camera roll: iOS's locked Hidden album syncs with iCloud Photos; see apps to hide photos.

How we checked

On 4 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. An app is here only if its listing says where the vault is backed up or synced, and it was updated in the last 12 months. We did not test a backup or a restore.

Order: first the backups that run by themselves, then the ones you control. See also photo vaults compared by privacy label and Calculator# alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

Do photo vault apps back up to iCloud?

Some do. Photo Vault and Converter keep an encrypted copy in your own iCloud, and Calculator# can back up to iCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive. Private Photo Vault and Keepsafe use their own cloud.

Will I lose my vault photos when I get a new iPhone?

You can, if the vault has no backup. Turn on the app's backup or export the vault before you reset the old phone, then restore on the new one.

Is the backup free?

Mostly not. Private Photo Vault's listing says the free version does not back up; PV's backups are paid plans; Photo Vault's iCloud copy needs Premium; Converter is a one-time purchase.

Which vault backup can only I open?

Photo Vault's iCloud copy is end-to-end encrypted in your own iCloud, and Converter's files are encrypted in your iCloud, per their listings. Private Photo Vault's Cloud Vault uses a separate password.
