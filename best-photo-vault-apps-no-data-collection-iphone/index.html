Best photo vault app for iPhone: no subscription trap, no lost photos (2026)

Updated 10 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 10 November 2026

For a photo vault with no subscription trap, pick one that lists a one-time purchase and keeps photos on your phone. On 10 November 2026 that is Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video (Premium Lifetime from $14.99) and Privault (Pro $45.99 once); Safe Lock is the only one of the six whose label says Data Not Collected. In 1-2 star reviews the most repeated complaints are being pushed to pay (66) and photos lost after a crash, update or new phone, so test a restore with one photo first. A privacy label is what the developer says, not an audit.

If you also want a private cloud, Keepsafe has the smallest label of the cloud options (usage data only); Private Photo Vault has the most features and the longest label.

Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video has no account to be locked out of and keeps photos on the device, with an optional end-to-end encrypted copy in your own iCloud (Premium). Safe Lock declares Data Not Collected.

"Unless you pay": photo vaults that charge after you add photos (66 of 395 low reviews)

We read the 395 one- and two-star US App Store reviews of the six apps below (from the latest 500 reviews of each). 66 are about being pushed to pay (Keepsafe 30, Safe Lock 13, Private Photo Vault 11, Privault 6). A Safe Lock reviewer wrote "Until your trial runs out..then, ANYONE can get your pics...unless you pay" (1 star).

The fix: before you move private photos in, look at what the free version protects and what the lock needs. On 10 November 2026 the listings show a one-time purchase in two of the six: Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video (Premium Lifetime $14.99 or $29.99) and Privault (Pro $45.99). A free option with no app at all is the Hidden album in Photos, locked with Face ID or a passcode on recent iOS versions.

"The app keeps crashing": 63 reviews

63 reviews say the vault crashes or no longer opens, 44 of them about Keepsafe, the biggest app here. "The app keeps crashing" and "Doesn't work with latest iOS version" (Keepsafe and Safe Lock, 1 star each) are typical.

The fix: update iOS and the app, restart the phone, and do not delete the app yet. Deleting a vault app can delete the vault with it. Use Settings > General > iPhone Storage > the app > Offload App, which keeps its data. Check the version date in the table: a vault that has not been updated for several iOS versions is the risky kind.

"Lost all my photos": 60 reviews

60 reviews say photos were lost after an update, a reinstall or a new phone (Keepsafe 36, PV 18). One Keepsafe reviewer wrote "Lost all my photos by using this as backup" (1 star); a Safe Lock reviewer says their folders were deleted after paying for lifetime.

The fix: never move the only copy. Keep the original in Photos until you have opened the vault copy on the phone, and test a restore with one photo. Make sure the vault is in your iPhone backup, or use an app with its own backup: Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video keeps photos on the device and offers an optional end-to-end encrypted copy in your own iCloud (Premium, off by default); Keepsafe, Private Photo Vault and PV have cloud plans.

"No backup" and "can't move to a new phone": 56 reviews

56 reviews are about backups that do not finish or a vault that does not come across to a new phone (Keepsafe 34, PV 12). Reviewers write that photos "do not back up" (Keepsafe, 2 stars).

The fix: move the vault with an iPhone-to-iPhone transfer or an encrypted backup, then open the vault on the new phone before you erase the old one. An app with no cloud copy, such as Safe Lock, only moves if the transfer includes app data, so check on the new phone first.

"Can't recover my password" and locked out: 41 reviews

41 reviews are about lost passwords, resets or accounts that cannot be recovered (Keepsafe 25, Safe Lock 5, Privault 5). "I can't recover or delete my account" is a 1-star Keepsafe review.

The fix depends on the trade-off: an app with an account can reset a password by email (set the recovery email now), while an app with no account has nothing to reset, so choose a code you will remember. Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video has no account and opens with Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode.

Who should use what

  • You want nothing collected and no account: Safe Lock; Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video if you accept a label with only purchases, not linked to you.
  • You want a private cloud with the smallest label: Keepsafe.
  • You want the most features and accept a long label: Private Photo Vault.
  • You want a decoy passcode for free: Privault (Photo Vault has one with Premium).
  • You want the cheapest subscription: PV.

Use cases

  • Keeping private photos off the shared camera roll and out of the cloud (Photo Vault, Safe Lock).
  • Handing your phone to someone without them seeing the app (disguised icons, decoy passcodes).
  • Backing up a vault so a lost phone does not lose photos (Keepsafe, Private Photo Vault, PV with their cloud).
  • Checking what an app says it collects before you put private photos in it (the privacy label in the table).

How we checked

On 10 November 2026 we opened each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data: in-app purchases, version and release date, rating, languages, size, minimum iOS and the privacy label exactly as declared. Features are the ones each developer lists on its page; we did not verify encryption or audit data practices. We did not invent ratings or reviews.

Complaint counts: we downloaded the latest 500 US App Store reviews of each app (2,501 in all) and read the 395 with one or two stars. Each was counted once per complaint by matching its words (for example subscri, pay, crash, lost, back up, password); a review can count in more than one group. The counts show how often a complaint comes up, not how often an app fails.

Frequently asked questions

Which photo vault apps say Data Not Collected?

On 9 November 2026, only Safe Lock - Hidden Photo Vault. Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video lists only purchases, not linked to you. Keepsafe, Private Photo Vault, Privault and PV list data used to track you or data linked to you.

Does a Data Not Collected label mean my photos are safe?

It means the developer declares to Apple that it does not collect data from the app. It is not an audit, and it says nothing about how well your photos are protected on the phone.

Which photo vault has a private cloud?

Keepsafe (Private Cloud, Premium), Private Photo Vault (Cloud Vault) and PV (cloud plans). Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video keeps photos on the device and offers an optional end-to-end encrypted copy in your own iCloud (Premium, off by default); Safe Lock keeps them on the device.

Is Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video the same as Private Photo Vault?

No. They are different apps by different developers. Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video is by Saga Ltd.; Private Photo Vault - Pic Safe is by Legendary Software Labs.

Which photo vault apps have no subscription?

On 10 November 2026 the US App Store pages list a one-time purchase in two of the six: Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video (Premium Lifetime at $14.99 or $29.99, next to Premium Yearly at $19.99 or $29.99) and Privault (Pro at $45.99, next to $3.49 a month or $24.99 a year). Safe Lock lists Premium Account at $4.99, $19.99 and $29.99 without saying which is one-time in the listing, so check the plan before you buy.

Do I need a vault app at all?

Not always. iPhone has a Hidden album in Photos you can lock with Face ID or a passcode on recent iOS versions; a vault app adds separate albums, disguised icons and other features.
