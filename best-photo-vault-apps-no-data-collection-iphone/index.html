Best photo vault apps with no data collection for iPhone in 2026

Updated 16 October 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 16 October 2026

Disclosure: Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video is made by Saga Labs, the company behind Reviewland. It is one more option in this list, not placed first because it is ours. It is a different app from "Private Photo Vault - Pic Safe", which is not ours. The other apps are not ours and we get nothing from them; their links go to their normal App Store pages.

Of the six, only two declare Data Not Collected on their App Store privacy label: Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video and Safe Lock. The other four list data used to track you, data linked to you, or both. A privacy label is what the developer says, not an independent audit.

If you also want a private cloud, Keepsafe has the smallest label of the cloud options (usage data only); Private Photo Vault has the most features and the longest label.

1

Safe Lock - Hidden Photo Vault

Best for: the other vault with a Data Not Collected label, with many lock types and a hidden camera.

  • Label: Data Not Collected. Locks: PIN, DotLock, numeric or alphabetic password, Touch ID and Face ID.
  • Hidden camera inside the app; secret folders for photos, videos and notes.
  • The developer says photos and videos are saved only on your device. 13 languages, iOS 12 or later, 69 MB.

Limits: its listing does not describe an encrypted export for moving to a new iPhone.

Secret Photo Vault: Keepsafe

Best for: a low-data label (usage data only) with an optional private cloud.

  • Label: usage data used to track you, and usage data not linked to you; no contact info or photos listed.
  • PIN, Touch ID, album lock with separate PINs (Premium), Safe Send.
  • 16 languages, iOS 17 or later, 210 MB.

Limits: its label includes tracking of usage data, and cloud and per-album locks need Premium.

3

Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video

Best for: the privacy label with nothing collected, plus Face ID, per-album passwords, disguised icon and a private camera.

  • Label: Data Not Collected. Opens with Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode; each album can have its own password; a decoy code (Premium) opens a separate vault.
  • Disguised icon (Calculator, Notes or Weather) and a face-down exit.
  • No account; an optional end-to-end encrypted copy in your own iCloud (off by default); to move to a new iPhone you export one password-locked file. 19 languages, iOS 17 or later, 17 MB.

Limits: no automatic backup unless you turn on the iCloud copy; the free version is limited (one item when we checked on 11 October); only 6 ratings in the US.

Private Photo Vault - Pic Safe

  • Break-in report with photo and GPS, decoy password, face-down lock, private browser.

Who should use what

  • You want nothing collected and no account: Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video or Safe Lock.
  • You want a private cloud with the smallest label: Keepsafe.
  • You want the most features and accept a long label: Private Photo Vault.
  • You want a decoy passcode for free: Privault (Photo Vault has one with Premium).
  • You want the cheapest subscription: PV.

Use cases

  • Keeping private photos off the shared camera roll and out of the cloud (Photo Vault, Safe Lock).
  • Handing your phone to someone without them seeing the app (disguised icons, decoy passcodes).
  • Backing up a vault so a lost phone does not lose photos (Keepsafe, Private Photo Vault, PV with their cloud).
  • Checking what an app says it collects before you put private photos in it (the privacy label in the table).

How we checked

On 16 October 2026 we opened each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data: in-app purchases, version and release date, rating and number of ratings, languages, size, minimum iOS and the privacy label exactly as declared. Features are the ones each developer lists on its page; we did not verify encryption or audit data practices. We did not invent ratings or reviews.

Frequently asked questions

Which photo vault apps say Data Not Collected?

On 16 October 2026, Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video and Safe Lock - Hidden Photo Vault. Keepsafe, Private Photo Vault, Privault and PV list data used to track you or data linked to you.

Does a Data Not Collected label mean my photos are safe?

It means the developer declares to Apple that it does not collect data from the app. It is not an audit, and it says nothing about how well your photos are protected on the phone.

Which photo vault has a private cloud?

Keepsafe (Private Cloud, Premium), Private Photo Vault (Cloud Vault) and PV (cloud plans). Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video and Safe Lock keep photos on the device.

Is Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video the same as Private Photo Vault?

No. They are different apps by different developers. Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video is by Saga Ltd.; Private Photo Vault - Pic Safe is by Legendary Software Labs.

Do I need a vault app at all?

Not always. iPhone has a Hidden album in Photos you can lock with Face ID or a passcode on recent iOS versions; a vault app adds separate albums, disguised icons and other features.
