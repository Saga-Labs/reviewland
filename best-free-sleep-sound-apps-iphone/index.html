Best free sleep sound apps for iPhone in 2026

Updated 16 October 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 16 October 2026

Disclosure: Drop is made by Saga Labs, the company behind Reviewland. It is one more option in this list, not placed first because it is ours. The other apps are not ours and we get nothing from them; their links go to their normal App Store pages.

For the most sound for free, White Noise Lite (50+ sounds, no purchases listed) and Rain Rain (50+ sounds, one small banner ad) are the strongest. For white, pink and brown noise on a free plan, White Noise Lite and White Noise Deep Sleep Sounds both list them. If you want free sounds with no ads at all and a sleep timer, Drop gives you 10 of its 32 sounds free, but no white noise.

All six are free to download. What differs is how much stays free, whether there are ads, and how long the sound plays; all of that is in the table.

1

White Noise Lite

Best for: the most free sound for no money: real noise colours, a mixer and timers.

  • 50+ looped sounds, including white, pink, brown, blue and violet noise.
  • Mix Pad to build mixes; background audio so you can use other apps.
  • Timers and alarms that fade audio in and out.
  • 3 languages (English, French, Spanish), iOS 14 or later, 94 MB.

Limits: no meditation or breathing, and its privacy label includes identifiers used to track you.

2

Rain Rain Sleep Sounds

Best for: lots of rain and nature sounds free with no time limit, if you accept a small banner ad.

  • Per its listing, the free version has 50+ HD sounds, no limit on playback time, every fade-out timer option and a mixer with per-sound volume.
  • No audio or video ads, only a small image banner at the bottom; Premium adds 60+ sounds and removes it.
  • 4 languages (English, French, Italian, Spanish), iOS 12 or later, 105 MB.

Limits: last updated on 22 January 2025, and its privacy label includes identifiers used to track you.

3

Sleep Sounds & Focus: Drop

Best for: free, ad-free calming sounds with a proper sleep timer and a short breathing guide.

  • 10 free sounds with no ad banner: rain, dewdrops, fire, forest, river, coffee shop, ocean waves, thunderstorm, bus and fan.
  • Sleep timer of 15, 30 or 45 minutes, 1, 2 or 8 hours, with a 30-second fade-out; works offline.
  • Breathing sessions of 2 to 20 minutes and a plant that grows as you practise.
  • 3 languages (English, Japanese, Chinese), iOS 18 or later, 204 MB.

Limits: only 10 sounds are free and the other 22 need Drop Pro; no white, pink or brown noise; no mixer; needs iOS 18 and has few ratings (12 in the US).

White Noise Deep Sleep Sounds

Best for: a big library (250+ extra premium sounds) with 30+ free sounds, including white, pink and brown noise, for adults and babies.

  • Free sounds listed include fan, baby shusher, vacuum cleaner, airplane cabin, rain, thunder, ocean waves, and white, pink and brown noise.
  • Sleep timer, sound mixing, widget, AirPlay 2.
  • Per its listing the free version plays sounds for eight hours; continuous play needs a subscription.
  • 33 languages, iOS 15 or later, 200 MB.

Limits: the free version stops after eight hours, so for a night of sleep it is enough, but not for all-day use; many price points.

Dark Noise: Ambient Sounds

Who should use what

  • You want the most free sound: White Noise Lite or Rain Rain.
  • You need white, pink or brown noise: White Noise Lite or White Noise Deep Sleep Sounds.
  • You want no ads and a good sleep timer: Drop.
  • You like a polished design and Siri Shortcuts, and have iOS 26: Dark Noise.
  • You want a classic app with alarm and mixes: Sleep Pillow, keeping in mind it was last updated in 2023.

Use cases

  • Falling asleep to rain or a fan with a timer that fades out (any of the six).
  • Masking street noise all night (White Noise Lite or Rain Rain, with no time limit).
  • Soothing a baby with white noise (White Noise Deep Sleep Sounds lists baby sounds and shushers).
  • Focus or study with a coffee shop or fan sound (Drop, White Noise Lite).

How we checked

On 16 October 2026 we opened each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data: in-app purchases, version and release date, rating and number of ratings, languages, size, minimum iOS and the privacy label. What is free comes from each developer's description; for Drop, which is ours, from the app itself (version 2.9). We did not invent ratings or reviews and did not test audio quality or battery use.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free sleep sound app for iPhone?

It depends on what you need free. White Noise Lite has 50+ sounds and no listed purchases; Rain Rain has 50+ free sounds with a small banner ad; Drop has 10 free sounds with no ads and a sleep timer.

Which free app has white noise, pink noise and brown noise?

White Noise Lite lists white, pink, brown, blue and violet noise; White Noise Deep Sleep Sounds lists white, pink and brown noise among its free sounds. Drop has a fan and a bus sound but not white, pink or brown noise.

Does the free version of a sleep sound app stop after a few hours?

White Noise Deep Sleep Sounds says its free version plays for eight hours and continuous play needs a subscription. Rain Rain lists no limit on playback time in its free version.

Is there a sleep sound app with no ads?

Drop has no ads. Rain Rain says it has no audio or video ads, only a small image banner. White Noise Lite lists no purchases; check its ads when you install it.

Which sleep sound app works on older iPhones?

Rain Rain and Sleep Pillow ask for iOS 12 or later, White Noise Lite iOS 14. Dark Noise needs iOS 26 and Drop needs iOS 18.
