ChatGPT video generator plugins compared

Updated 2 November 2026 · Plugins and prices last verified 2 November 2026

Last verified 2 November 2026. We read the listings of nine plugins that make or edit video from ChatGPT, and each vendor's own pricing page. For general text to video, start with Runway or OpenArt. For a longer video from one prompt, invideo. For a presenter, HeyGen or VEED. To edit a recording, Descript. We did not install or run any of them, so this page says what each listing and pricing page claims, not how good the videos are.

Free plans: Runway (125 credits once), Higgsfield, VEED, HeyGen (3 watermarked videos a month) and Descript. The cheapest paid plans start at $10 a month billed yearly (invideo, VEED). Each plugin uses its own account; OpenAI says availability depends on the plugin, your plan and your workspace settings. For other kinds of plugins, see our wider guide to ChatGPT plugins.

1

Runway

Best for: generating and also editing video from one chat.

  • The listing covers animating images, targeted edits to footage, new aspect ratios, multi-shot story videos and localising ads.
  • It also lists music, sound effects, voiceover, and a check of remaining credits and plan.
  • Runway's pricing page: Free with 125 credits once; Standard 625 credits a month; Pro $35, Max $95 a month.

Limits: you need a Runway account, and the free credits are a one-off, not monthly.

OpenArt

Best for: picking a specific model, such as Seedance or Wan.

  • The listing names Seedance 2.5 and 2.0, Minimax H3, Gemini Omni Flash and Wan 3.0, among others.
  • OpenArt's pricing page: Starter 4,000 credits a month, Plus $34, Pro $56, Wonder $240 a month.
  • The result comes back into the chat, and past work stays in projects.

Limits: no editing tools: the listing says none are required, and it does not describe any.

Higgsfield

Best for: short ads made from a product photo.

  • Marketing Studio presets turn product photos into promotional visuals and short video ads.
  • Genjutsu transfers motion from a reference video, or replaces a subject in a clip.
  • Higgsfield's pricing page: Starter 270 credits a month; Plus $47 and Ultra $99 a month billed yearly.

Limits: built mostly around marketing, and it needs a connected account.

invideo

Best for: a longer video from one prompt.

  • Its tagline is "Create videos of any length", and its sample prompt asks for a 5-minute video.
  • invideo's pricing page: Starter 400 credits a month; Plus $60 a month ($30 yearly); Max $250 a month.

Limits: the listing does not state a maximum length, and the cheapest plan has 400 credits a month.

5

Dora AI Video

Best for: a short vertical clip from a photo.

  • The listing says videos arrive in about two minutes and images in about one.
  • Vertical, horizontal or square, and the same character across a series of clips.
  • Dora's pricing page (re-read 4 Nov 2026): Pro weekly or yearly, both with 150 coins a week, and top-up packs when you need more.

Limits: short clips only, and no free plan. It does not publish to social networks for you. We found no OpenAI business page for it, so we read its ChatGPT directory listing instead.

VEED Video Generator

Best for: a presenter video with no camera.

  • You choose from a library of characters and voices.
  • VEED's pricing page: Creator 6,000 credits a month with no watermark; Pro $44 and Studio $70 a month ($21 and $35 yearly).

Limits: the listing does not describe other video styles, and the pricing page does not say what the free plan includes.

7

HeyGen

Best for: talking avatars and translated videos from a script.

  • Clips can open in a player in the chat, on supported hosts.
  • You can manage videos, avatars, voices, translations, templates and brand assets from the chat.
  • HeyGen's pricing page: Pro $49 a month; Business $149 a month plus $20 per seat.

Limits: features depend on your plan, and the listing says creating an avatar or voice requires the appropriate rights and consent.

8

Descript

Best for: editing a recording rather than generating new footage.

  • Transcription, filler word removal, Studio Sound, captions, B-roll and clips.
  • Descript's pricing page: the free plan also has 100 AI credits once; Creator $35 a month ($24 yearly).

Limits: it is a video editor first, so it is not the obvious choice for generating scenes.

9

Kling AI

Best for: technical users who already work in Codex.

  • Text to video, image to video, motion control and reusable character Elements, through the official Kling CLI.
  • Kling's web plan page asks you to sign in, so the price here is from its App Store page: Standard $10, Pro $37, Premier $92 a month.

Limits: the listing requires a local Codex environment with Node.js 18+, Kling CLI 0.2.0+ and shell access, so it is not a simple option for most people.

Who should use what

  • General text to video, plus editing: Runway.
  • Choosing the model: OpenArt.
  • Product ads: Higgsfield.
  • A longer video from one prompt: invideo.
  • A short clip from a photo: Dora AI Video.
  • A presenter with no camera: VEED or HeyGen.
  • Editing a recording: Descript.
  • Working in Codex: Kling AI.

Use cases

  • A product clip from one photo.
  • A narrated explainer from a written brief.
  • Cleaning up and dubbing a recorded webinar.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a video in ChatGPT?

Install a video plugin, connect its account, and start a chat asking it to use the plugin. OpenAI's listings describe these steps, and some connections need admin approval.

Are ChatGPT video plugins free?

Some have free plans on their own sites: Runway (125 credits once), Higgsfield, VEED, HeyGen (3 watermarked videos a month) and Descript. OpenArt, invideo and Dora AI Video show no free plan.

What is the cheapest ChatGPT video plugin?

On 2 November 2026 the lowest paid plans were invideo Starter and VEED Creator at $10 a month billed yearly ($20 monthly), then Runway Standard at $12 a month billed yearly.

Which plugin makes the best videos?

We do not know, because we did not test them. Try the same short prompt in two plugins, on the free plan or the smallest paid option each offers.

Do I need a ChatGPT Business plan?

The pages we read are on OpenAI's business site and mention ChatGPT Work and Codex. They say availability depends on the plugin, your plan and your workspace settings, without listing plans per plugin.
