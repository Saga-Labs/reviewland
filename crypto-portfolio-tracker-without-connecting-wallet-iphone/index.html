Track your crypto portfolio on iPhone without connecting a wallet or exchange

Updated 9 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

You don't need to connect a wallet, an exchange or an API key to follow what your crypto is worth: these apps let you type in the coins and amounts you hold and price them live. For the most complete app that keeps your data on your devices, Crypto Pro. For no account at all and one clear number, BitWorth. For a manual portfolio inside a big research app, CoinGecko. For a free, ad-free portfolio with profit and loss and a widget, Crypton.

A wallet address is public, but pasting it into an app links your holdings to that app; an exchange API key gives an app access to your account. Typing amounts by hand shares neither. The trade-off: you update the amounts yourself after each buy or sale.

1

Crypto Pro - Live Coin Tracker

Best for: the most complete tracker that still keeps your data on your own devices.

  • Its listing says personal data is stored on your device, not on its servers, with no user monitoring or analytics; optional iCloud backup and sync.
  • Also on iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, with widgets and price notifications.
  • Tracks precious metals and fiat currencies as well as crypto.

Limits: automatic import from addresses and exchanges is a Premium feature, and the listing does not say what else the free version limits.

BitWorth - Crypto Tracker

Best for: one number, what your crypto is worth now, with nothing to sign up for.

  • Its listing says you open the app, add the coins you hold and see their value in your currency; everything stays on the device unless you back it up.
  • Built by one developer; has a live global chat and news feed if you want them.

Limits: needs iOS 26, English only, and it is deliberately simple: no P&L by buy price is mentioned in the listing.

CoinGecko: Crypto Tracker

Best for: a manual portfolio next to the biggest free price and research database.

  • Its listing says you build your own portfolio and track real-time profit and loss based on your transactions.
  • Over 10,000 coins, NFT floor prices, categories, price alerts and widgets.

Limits: a big research app more than a portfolio tool, English only, and its privacy label includes data linked to you.

Crypto Tracker: Crypton

Best for: a free, ad-free portfolio you fill in yourself, with P&L and a Home Screen widget.

Who should use what

  • Everything on your own devices, with iPad, Mac and Watch: Crypto Pro.
  • No account, just the total: BitWorth.
  • A portfolio next to research and alerts: CoinGecko.
  • Free, no ads, P&L by buy price and a widget: Crypton.

Use cases

  • You hold coins on a hardware wallet and don't want to paste its address anywhere: type the amounts into any of the five.
  • You want the total on your Home Screen: Crypto Pro, CoinGecko, Crypton and BitScreener list widgets; see crypto widget apps.
  • You hold coins on several exchanges and want them to update by themselves: that needs syncing; see CoinStats and Delta alternatives.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. An app is here only if its listing says you can add holdings by hand or needs no exchange hookup, and it was updated in 2026. We did not test accuracy of prices.

Frequently asked questions

Can I track crypto without connecting my wallet?

Yes. Crypto Pro, BitWorth, CoinGecko, Crypton and BitScreener let you add the coins and amounts by hand, and they price them live.

Which crypto tracker needs no account?

BitWorth says no account and no sign-up. Crypton's account is optional, only to sync the portfolio. Crypto Pro keeps data on the device with optional iCloud sync. CoinMarketCap's listing asks you to sign up to make a portfolio.

Is it safe to paste my wallet address into a tracker?

An address only lets an app read public balances, not move coins, but it links that wallet to you in the app. Never type a seed phrase or private key into a tracker.

Which of these collect the least data?

Crypto Pro and BitWorth show only Data Not Linked to You on their App Store privacy labels. Crypton and CoinGecko also list some data linked to you; for Crypton, purchases and identifiers (rechecked on 9 November 2026).
