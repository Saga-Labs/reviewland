How to make an AI video on iPhone, step by step

Updated 2 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 2 November 2026

1. Decide what you start from: a sentence, a photo, or clips you filmed. 2. Pick an app that does that: the table below shows which. 3. Choose the shape first: vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels or Shorts. 4. Keep it short, because longer clips usually cost more. 5. Check the cost before you generate. 6. Look at the result and change one thing at a time. 7. Save it, and add captions in an editor if you post it.

For a first try, write one sentence with who, what happens and where, such as: a grey cat walks along a rainy window ledge at night, slow camera move. Long scripts need an app that builds several scenes.

Who should use what

  • Best resolution and length on a listing: Kling AI.
  • Several models or a whole video: Runway.
  • A first short clip with templates: Dora.
  • Inside a design: Canva.
  • A talking person: Captions.

Use cases

  • A clip from one sentence.
  • An old photo that moves.
  • A short ad for a small shop.

How we checked

On 2 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. What each app does comes from its own listing. We ran no quality test, so we do not say which one looks best.

Prices change often. Check inside each app before paying.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an AI video take on iPhone?

Dora and Runway both say about two minutes on their listings. Longer or higher-resolution clips can take more.

Do I need editing skills?

No, for one clip. To join several clips and add captions you need a simple editor afterwards, such as Edits or VN.

Which iPhone do I need?

Check the minimum on each listing: Dora needs iOS 18.6, Captions iOS 16, Runway iOS 16.4, Canva iOS 15.4 and Kling iOS 14.
