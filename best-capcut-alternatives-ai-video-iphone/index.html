Best CapCut alternatives for AI video on iPhone

Updated 2 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 2 November 2026

Which alternative fits depends on what you use CapCut for. To edit clips you filmed, start with Edits or VN, both free according to their listings. To make video from a sentence, CapCut's own listing does not describe that, so look at Canva, Filmora or Dora.

For reference, CapCut itself is at version 19.9.0 (27 October 2026), rated 4.6 from over 1.1 million ratings, with Standard at $9.99 a month and Pro at $19.99.

1

Edits: Video Editor

Best for: the closest free replacement if you post to Instagram.

  • Exports in 4K with no watermark, per its listing.
  • Automatic captions, green screen and AI animation of images.
  • A dashboard with how your reels perform.

Limits: built around Instagram, and its label links data to you. It does not list text to video.

2

VN: Photo & Video Editor

Best for: a timeline editor without paying.

  • Its listing says free with no watermark.
  • Multi-track timeline, auto captions and text to speech.
  • Removes filler words and silences from talking clips.

Limits: AI extras run on credits, and it does not list text to video.

App Store page →

3

InShot - Video Editor

Best for: a one-time payment instead of a subscription.

  • The only lifetime price on this list: $49.99.
  • AI Cut removes silence and filler words.
  • Speed from 0.1x to 100x, keyframes and bilingual subtitles.

Limits: it improves videos you already have; its listing does not offer text to video.

App Store page →

4

Canva AI Photo & Video Editor

Best for: making video from text inside a design app.

  • Its listing offers Veo 3 video with sound from a text prompt.
  • Video editor with captions and Beat Sync, plus templates for everything else you design.

Limits: general design first, and credits or Pro for AI work.

App Store page →

5

Filmora: AI Video Editor&Maker

Best for: one app that both edits and generates.

  • Lists Image to Video, Text to Video, AI Auto Cut and Dynamic Captions.

Limits: AI credits are sold separately, and its privacy label lists all three data categories.

App Store page →

6

DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: generating the clip you would otherwise film, then finishing it in an editor.

  • About two minutes a video, up to 1080p, no watermark, per its listing.
  • Shows the coin cost before you generate.

Limits: it is not an editor: no timeline, so it does not replace CapCut's editing. No free video.

Who should use what

  • Free editing for Instagram: Edits.
  • A free timeline: VN.
  • Pay once: InShot.
  • Text to video with design tools: Canva.
  • Editing and generating together: Filmora.
  • A generated clip to finish elsewhere: Dora.

Use cases

  • Replacing CapCut for captions and quick cuts.
  • Avoiding a monthly plan.
  • Adding generated shots to a video you filmed.

How we checked

On 2 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. What each app does comes from its own listing. We ran no quality test, so we do not say which one looks best.

Prices change often. Check inside each app before paying.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free alternative to CapCut on iPhone?

Edits and VN both describe themselves as free with no watermark on their listings. Edits lists no purchases; VN sells a Pro plan and credits.

Which CapCut alternative makes AI video from text?

On this list Canva, Filmora and Dora describe that on their listings. Edits, VN and InShot do not.

Does Dora replace CapCut?

No. Dora generates clips and has no timeline. You would still edit in another app.
