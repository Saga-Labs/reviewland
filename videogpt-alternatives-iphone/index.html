VideoGPT alternatives for iPhone

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

VideoGPT (by ElevenThirteen) turns a written story into a video in styles from Cinematic to Anime, but its last App Store update was version 4.7 on 8 October 2025. All six apps here were updated in the last month. For a whole story with narration and music, invideo. For sharp clips up to 4K and scenes extended to 3 minutes, Kling AI. For a quick clip with sound inside a chat app, Grok. For short clips from a written idea or a photo, with the cost shown first, Dora.

All six are free to download and sell subscriptions or credits; none of these listings promises free video. VideoGPT itself lists Unlimited at $6.99 a week or $69.99 a year and Pro at $14.99 a week.

1

invideo: AI Video Editor

Best for: a story told from start to finish, with narration and music, closest to what VideoGPT promises.

  • Its listing says you can start from an idea, a script or your own footage, and an AI agent (Agent Two) builds and edits the video.
  • Generates images, video, voice, sound and music with 200+ AI models, on a timeline editor.

Limits: the plans cost more than a weekly clip app, and the app is English only.

2

Kling AI: AI Image&Video Maker

Best for: sharp clips from a prompt, and longer scenes by extending a clip.

  • Its listing says text and images become video, up to 15 seconds in native 1080p or 4K, and Video Extension takes a clip up to 3 minutes.
  • Updated on 2 November 2026, the most recent here.

Limits: the listing does not say how many credits a video costs, and it is in 5 languages.

App Store page →

3

Grok

Best for: a quick clip with sound inside a chat app you may already pay for.

  • Its listing says Grok Imagine makes short videos with sound and turns photos into videos.
  • The most ratings here, over 1.5 million, and 38 languages.

Limits: video is one feature of a chat app, and the listing gives no clip length or cost per video.

App Store page →

4

DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: short clips from a written idea or a photo, with the coin cost shown before you tap.

  • Its listing says you write an idea or add a photo and get a video back in about two minutes.
  • The coin cost shows before you generate, and failed generations give the coins back, per its listing.
  • Dora's site lists Seedance 2 Mini as the default model: 8 seconds with sound for 31 coins; Kling 2.6 and Grok are also there.

Limits: short clips only, no story editor, and a subscription with no free video.

App Store page →

5

Pollo AI: AI Video & Image

Best for: trying many video models, including Veo 3.1, from one app.

  • Its listing says ideas, scripts, photos, product images, URLs or reference videos become finished videos for ads, social media and stories.
  • Names Seedance, Veo 3.1, Kling, Hailuo, Vidu and PixVerse among its models.

Limits: English only, and the listing does not say what each model costs.

App Store page →

6

Runway: AI Image & Video

Best for: describing a video in plain words and letting an agent plan several shots.

  • Its listing names Seedance 2.5, Kling 3 Pro and Gen-4.5 among its models, and says the first generation takes about two minutes.
  • Runway Agent turns a description into several shots and assembles them.

Limits: English only, no iPad version, and the listing gives no clip length or sound.

Who should use what

  • A whole story with narration and music: invideo.
  • Sharp clips and longer scenes: Kling AI.
  • A clip with sound from a chat: Grok.
  • Short clips from an idea or a photo, cost shown first: Dora.
  • Many models in one app: Pollo AI.
  • Several shots planned for you: Runway.

Use cases

  • A bedtime story or a short tale as a video with a narrator: invideo.
  • An anime or cinematic scene from one prompt: Kling AI, Dora or Pollo AI; name the style in the prompt.
  • A product clip for social media: Pollo AI or Dora.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data, and for Kling and Dora the company's own site. An app is here only if its listing says it makes video from text and it was updated in the last 30 days. We generated nothing and did not compare quality.

Order: first the apps that make longer or more finished videos, then the clip apps. See also text-to-video apps, AI video apps with sound and Grok Imagine alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

Is VideoGPT still updated?

Its App Store page shows version 4.7, released on 8 October 2025, and nothing newer on 3 November 2026. The app is still on sale.

What is the best alternative to VideoGPT?

For whole story videos with narration, invideo. For the sharpest clips, Kling AI. For short clips with the cost shown before you tap, Dora.

Is there a free alternative to VideoGPT?

None of these listings promises free video. They sell subscriptions, weekly plans or credits; Grok's video comes with its SuperGrok plans.

Which alternatives make video with sound?

Grok and invideo say so in their listings, Dora on the models that support it, and Pollo AI on some models. Kling's site says Kling 4.0 makes sound; its listing does not.
