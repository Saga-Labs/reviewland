Hide it Pro alternatives for iPhone

Updated 4 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 4 November 2026

Hide it Pro hides photos behind a lock screen and can look as if it crashed, but its iPhone app was last updated on 11 January 2026 and is in English only. All six here were updated since August 2026. For the closest match with no data collected and a fake gallery, Safe Lock. For the best-known vault with an encrypted backup, Private Photo Vault. For a vault synced across devices, Keepsafe. For no account and a backup only in your own iCloud, Photo Vault.

Before you switch, export your photos from Hide it Pro (its listing names USB export and unhide to the gallery), then import them into the new vault.

1

Safe Lock - Hidden Photo Vault

Best for: the closest to Hide it Pro in size and privacy, with more ways to fool a snooper.

  • Its listing says photos are saved only on the device, with a fake password that opens a fake gallery and break-in reports with photos.
  • Custom app icon; export to iCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive or Box; Wi-Fi transfer.

Limits: no automatic cloud copy: you export yourself.

2

Private Photo Vault - Pic Safe

Best for: the best-known vault, updated this week, with an encrypted cloud backup.

  • About 1 million ratings; updated on 2 November 2026.
  • Encrypted Cloud Vault backup (paid) and a private browser, per its listing.

Limits: its privacy label includes data used to track you, and the free version has no cloud backup.

3

Secret Photo Vault: Keepsafe

Best for: the same vault on several devices, synced automatically.

  • Its listing says Private Cloud stores up to 10,000 items and syncs to all your Keepsafe installs.
  • Trash recovery for photos deleted by mistake; 16 languages.

Limits: the cloud is Keepsafe's, and its label includes data used to track you.

App Store page →

4

Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video

Best for: no account and nothing uploaded unless you choose, updated this month.

  • Its listing says each album can have its own password, the icon can look like Calculator, Notes or Weather, and flipping the phone jumps to another app.
  • Optional end-to-end encrypted copy in your own iCloud; privacy label Data Not Collected.

Limits: the free version is limited (Premium adds unlimited photos and the iCloud copy).

5

PV - Secret Photo Album

Best for: the most features for the money, with Wi-Fi transfer like Hide it Pro's USB export.

  • Its listing names a fake password, custom app icon, Emergency Switch, trash folder and Wi-Fi transfer to a computer.
  • Free unlimited storage on the phone; ads until you pay.

Limits: backups and the fake password are paid, and its privacy label is the widest here.

SPV - Secret Photo Vault

Best for: keeping contacts and passwords in the same locked app.

  • Its listing says it stores photos, videos, passwords, contacts and browser data behind a PIN.
  • About 70,000 ratings.

Limits: no decoy or disguise named in the listing, and its label includes data used to track you.

Who should use what

  • Closest match, no data collected: Safe Lock.
  • Best-known, encrypted backup: Private Photo Vault.
  • Synced across devices: Keepsafe.
  • No account, backup in your own iCloud: Photo Vault.
  • Most features, Wi-Fi transfer: PV.
  • Contacts and passwords too: SPV.

Use cases

  • Moving your albums out of Hide it Pro: unhide them to the gallery, import them into the new vault, then delete them from Photos and from Recently Deleted.
  • Someone asks to open the app: a fake password (Safe Lock, PV) or decoy password (Private Photo Vault) shows other photos.
  • You also want the vault to survive a new iPhone: see photo vaults with a backup.

How we checked

On 4 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. An app is here only if it is a photo vault updated since August 2026. We did not test how each lock holds up.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hide it Pro still updated on iPhone?

Its App Store page shows version 6.3.0 from 11 January 2026 and nothing newer on 4 November 2026. The app is still on sale.

What is the best alternative to Hide it Pro?

Safe Lock is the closest, with no data collected and a fake gallery. Private Photo Vault is the best known. Photo Vault needs no account and keeps the backup in your own iCloud.

Which alternative has a fake password?

Safe Lock (opens a fake gallery), PV (paid) and Private Photo Vault (decoy password), per their listings.

How do I move my photos out of Hide it Pro?

Its listing names unhide to the phone gallery and USB export. Unhide them, import them into the new vault, then delete them from Photos and Recently Deleted.
