Best AI apps to make a family photo talk on iPhone

Updated 2 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 2 November 2026

For a short talking message from a photo, Avatarify or Revive do this and use your own recording. For an old family portrait where movement is enough, MyHeritage is built around family history, though its listing does not promise speech. Dora only does talking on the web.

Before you animate someone, ask them, or the closest family if they have died. A voice or face made to say words someone never said can upset people. Say that it was made with AI when you share it, and do not use it to imitate someone to a person who could believe it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a photo of a relative who has died talk?

Apps can do it, but it is a personal decision. Talk to the closest family first, and avoid putting words in their mouth that they never said. MyHeritage's movement without speech is a gentler option.

Should I clone a relative's voice?

Only with their clear permission. It is safer to record your own voice or to have them record the message, which Avatarify and Revive's listings allow for.

Which photo works best?

One clear face looking towards the camera is the usual advice. Results depend on the app, and we did not test them.

Does the Dora iPhone app make photos talk?

No. The iPhone app animates photos without speech. Lip Sync is on doravideo.com, with an audio file of up to 15 seconds.
