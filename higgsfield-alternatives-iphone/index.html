Higgsfield alternatives for iPhone

Updated 4 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 4 November 2026

Higgsfield has no app on the App Store: a search for it on the US store on 4 November 2026 found none, and higgsfield.ai links to no iPhone app. You can use it in Safari. If you want an app, pick by what you use Higgsfield for. For cinematic clips like Cinema Studio, Kling AI. For many models in one place, including Seedance 2.5, Runway. For product ads like Ads Studio, Pollo AI. For a short clip from a photo or an idea, with the cost shown first, Dora. For viral presets like Higgsfield Shorts, Hula AI or PixVerse Lite.

All six are free to download and sell plans, credits or coins; none of these listings promises free video.

Kling AI: AI Image&Video Maker

Best for: cinematic clips in 1080p or 4K, and scenes extended up to 3 minutes.

  • Its listing says text and images become video, up to 15 seconds in native 1080p or 4K, extendable to 3 minutes.
  • A community feed to reuse other creators' ideas; updated on 2 November 2026.

Limits: one family of models (Kling's own), 5 languages, and the listing does not say how many credits a clip costs.

Runway: AI Image & Video

Best for: the closest to Higgsfield's many-models idea: Seedance 2.5, Kling 3 Pro and Gen-4.5 in one app.

  • Its listing names Seedance 2.5, Kling 3 Pro, Gen-4.5, Nano Banana Pro and Nano Banana 2.

Pollo AI: AI Video & Image

Best for: product ads and marketing videos from a photo, a script or a URL.

DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: a short clip from a photo or a written idea, with the coin cost shown before you tap.

  • Dora's site lists Seedance 2 Mini as the default model, plus Kling 2.6, Gemini Omni and Grok.
