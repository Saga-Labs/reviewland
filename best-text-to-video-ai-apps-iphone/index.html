Best text-to-video AI apps for iPhone

Updated 2 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 2 November 2026

If you have a script or a description and no photo, these seven iPhone apps say they turn text alone into video. For one sharp clip, Kling AI (up to 15 seconds in 1080p or 4K). To pick the model or let an agent plan several shots, Runway. For a whole edited video with voice and music from a long script, invideo, or Faceless for a quick vertical reel.

None of them makes video free in its listing, and most do not say what one video costs. Paste one shot at a time, choose 9:16 for a phone, and look at the cost before you generate. For the steps, see how to turn text into a video on iPhone.

1

Kling AI: AI Image&Video Maker

Best for: the longest and sharpest single clips from a text prompt.

  • Its listing says text and images become video, up to 15 seconds in native 1080p or 4K.
  • Video Extension takes a clip up to 3 minutes.
  • A community feed lets you copy the prompt behind other people's videos.

Limits: the listing does not say how many credits a video costs, and it does not mention sound.

2

Runway: AI Image & Video

Best for: choosing the model, or letting an agent turn a description into several shots.

  • The listing names Seedance 2.5, Kling 3 Pro and Gen-4.5 among its video models.
  • Runway Agent plans the shots, generates them and assembles the video from a plain description.
  • It says the first generation takes about two minutes.

Limits: English only, and the listing gives no clip length or cost per video.

Grok

Best for: people who already pay for Grok and want a short clip with sound.

  • Its listing says Grok Imagine makes images from text, short videos with sound, and videos from photos.
  • 38 languages, the most of any app here.

Limits: video is one feature of a chat app, and the listing gives no length or cost per video.

4

DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: one short clip from a description, with the coin cost shown before you tap.

  • Type what happens in the shot, choose 9:16 and generate; the coin cost shows before you tap. In version 1.4.2 the default model is still named "SeeDance 1.5 Pro" in the picker; Dora's site says those requests now run on Seedance 2 Mini, at 31 coins for 8 seconds with sound.
  • At the 300-coin pack price that is about $0.41 per 8-second clip.
  • Videos up to 1080p on some models, no watermark, about two minutes per video, per its listing.

Limits: short clips only, and no free video.

invideo: AI Video Editor

Best for: turning a long script into an edited video with voice and music.

  • Start from an idea, a script or your own footage, and Agent Two develops it into a video, per its listing.
  • 200+ models for image, video, voice, sound and music, and a timeline editor.

Limits: English only, the most expensive plans here, and more editing than most people need for one clip.

Faceless - AI Video Generator

Best for: a faceless reel for TikTok or Shorts straight from a script.

  • Its listing says you paste text or a script and it picks visuals, music and transitions.
  • 33 languages, and the only app here whose privacy label has no tracking or linked data.

Limits: a small developer with about 1,200 ratings, and the look comes from its own templates.

VideoGPT - AI Video Generator

Best for: a story told in one visual style, such as anime.

  • Many ratings for its size: 4.8 from about 55,700.

Who should use what

  • One sharp clip: Kling AI.
  • Choosing the model, or several shots from one description: Runway.
  • You already pay for Grok: Grok Imagine.
  • One short clip with the cost shown first: Dora.
  • A long script with voice and music: invideo.
  • A vertical reel from a script: Faceless.
  • A story in one style, such as anime: VideoGPT.

Use cases

  • A 30-second explainer from a written script: invideo or Faceless split it into scenes.
  • One cinematic shot from a sentence: Kling AI, Runway or Dora.
  • A clip with sound from a chat: Grok.

How we checked

On 2 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. An app is on this list only if its listing says it makes video from text alone. What each one does, how long a clip is and whether there is sound come from the listing; we generated nothing and ran no quality test.

Google Gemini is left out because its listing does not mention video. CapCut and Filmora are editors first, so they are not here. The list is ordered by how directly each listing describes text to video, not by quality. For apps compared on sound, see AI video generator apps with sound.

Frequently asked questions

Which app turns a script into a video on iPhone?

For a long script with voice and music, invideo; for a short vertical reel, Faceless. For one scene at a time, Kling AI, Runway or Dora.

Can I make a video from text without a photo?

Yes. All seven apps here say they accept text alone. Kling AI, Runway, Grok and Dora also accept a photo if you have one.

Is there a free text-to-video app?

All are free to download, but none of these listings promises free videos. They sell subscriptions or credits.

How long can the video be?

Kling AI lists up to 15 seconds per clip, extendable to 3 minutes. Dora's default model makes 8 seconds. The others do not state a length.

Which apps add sound?

Grok says its short videos have sound, invideo generates voice, sound and music, Faceless adds music, and Dora's default model includes sound. Kling AI's listing does not mention sound.
