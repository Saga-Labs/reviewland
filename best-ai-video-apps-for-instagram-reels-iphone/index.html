Best AI video apps for Instagram Reels on iPhone

Updated 2 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 2 November 2026

If you film your Reels, start with Edits, Instagram's own free editor, or CapCut for finer control. If you talk to camera, Captions is built for that. If you have nothing filmed, Canva, Dora or Kling generate video from text.

None of these apps can promise reach. Edits is the only one here whose listing shows Reels performance inside the app.

1

Edits: Video Editor

Best for: Reels from clips you filmed, from Instagram itself.

  • Captures clips up to 10 minutes and exports in 4K with no watermark.
  • Reels performance and trending audio inside the app.
  • AI animation of images and automatic captions.

Limits: it does not list text to video, and its label links data to you.

2

CapCut: Photo & Video Editor

Best for: detailed editing, with Instagram named as a destination on its listing.

  • Keyframes, auto captions, text to speech and background removal.
  • Speed from 0.1x to 100x.

Limits: the listing describes editing tools rather than generating video from a prompt; its label includes tracking and data linked to you.

Canva AI Photo & Video Editor

Best for: Reels that match your other designs.

  • Its listing names social media reels and offers Veo 3 video with sound from text.
  • Captions and Beat Sync in the editor.

Limits: AI work needs credits or Pro, and the app covers far more than video.

Captions: AI Edits Your Video

Best for: talking-to-camera Reels, or an avatar instead of filming.

  • A chat-based editor applies cuts, B-roll and effects from what you describe.
  • AI Twin makes a video of you from one selfie, per its listing.
  • AI Scale resizes videos for other aspect ratios.

Limits: the avatar look may not suit every account; we did not test it.

DORA · AI Video Generator

Best for: a vertical clip for a Reel with nothing filmed.

  • 9:16 for Reels, up to 1080p, no watermark, per its listing.
  • About two minutes a video, with the coin cost shown first.

Limits: it does not publish to Instagram or edit on a timeline. No free video.

Kling AI: AI Image&Video Maker

Best for: longer generated scenes.

  • Up to 15 seconds in 1080p or 4K, extendable to 3 minutes, per its listing.
  • A community feed to clone ideas from.

Limits: credits go quickly on longer clips, and only 5 languages.

Who should use what

  • You film, and want it free: Edits.
  • You film, and edit in detail: CapCut.
  • On-brand designs: Canva.
  • Talking to camera or an avatar: Captions.
  • Nothing filmed, one short clip: Dora.
  • Nothing filmed, longer scenes: Kling AI.

Use cases

  • A faceless Reel from one sentence.
  • Captions on a clip you filmed.
  • A product clip for a small shop's account.

How we checked

On 2 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. What each app does comes from its own listing. We ran no quality test, so we do not say which one looks best.

Prices change often. Check inside each app before paying.

Frequently asked questions

What size should an AI video for Reels be?

Vertical 9:16. Choose it before you generate: resizing a horizontal clip later cuts off the sides.

Can these apps post to Instagram for me?

Edits lists easy sharing to Instagram. Dora does not publish for you: you save the clip and upload it yourself.

Is there a free way to make Reels with AI?

Edits is free according to its listing and includes AI animation of images and automatic captions. It does not list text to video.
