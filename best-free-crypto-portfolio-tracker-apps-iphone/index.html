Best free crypto portfolio trackers for iPhone in 2026

Updated 16 October 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 16 October 2026

Disclosure: Crypton is made by Saga Labs, the company behind Reviewland. It is one more option in this list, not placed first because it is ours. The other apps are not ours and we get nothing from them; their links go to their normal App Store pages. None of this is financial advice.

The free plans differ in one way that matters: limits. Delta and HODL cap the free tier at 10 assets or symbols; CoinGecko lists no cap and a manual portfolio; CoinMarketCap needs a sign-up for the portfolio; Crypto Pro does not say what is free; Crypton has no limits and no ads, but it is a simple manual list with no wallet sync and no alerts.

If you only need to see your holdings and the top coins, any of them works. If you want automatic syncing with exchanges and wallets for free, Delta's free plan is the closest, within its 10-asset limit.

1

Crypto Pro - Live Coin Tracker

Best for: privacy and Apple devices: no ads, data stored on your device, Mac menu bar and Apple Watch.

  • Imports holdings from wallet addresses or exchange API keys; profit and loss.
  • The listing says no analytics or user monitoring and an optional iCloud sync; no ads.
  • Widgets, Face ID lock, Siri shortcuts; 66 languages, iOS 15 or later, 104 MB.

Limits: the listing does not say exactly what the free tier includes, so check before relying on it.

Delta by etoro

Best for: crypto and stocks together, with automatic syncing, on a free plan capped at 10 assets.

  • Syncs 200+ exchanges, brokers and wallets; stocks, ETFs, forex and 10,000+ coins.
  • Free plan: 10 assets, unlimited connections, price alerts and 3 AI uses a month.
  • 18 languages, iOS 16.4 or later, 131 MB.

Limits: the free plan stops at 10 assets, and its privacy label includes data used to track you.

Crypton: Crypto Tracker

Best for: a free, ad-free look at the top coins and your holdings, with no limits to hit.

  • Portfolio with the amounts you enter, value and change per coin, and price charts (1D to 1Y).
  • Favourites and a watchlist; 250+ coins per the listing.
  • Account optional, no ads, nothing to buy. English only, iOS 18 or later, 19 MB.

Limits: no wallet or exchange connection, no price alerts, no widgets; prices refresh every few hours, so it is not for trading; charts load from Binance, which blocks US connections.

HODL Bitcoin & Crypto Tracker

Best for: crypto and US stocks in one watchlist, with news and social posts.

  • Free: watchlist and portfolio of up to 10 symbols, prices, charts, news, social posts and a widget.
  • Premium: unlimited symbols and price alerts with smart brackets, plus Wall Street research summaries.
  • Pro adds an AI agent called Hermes, per the listing. English only, iOS 15.1 or later, 79 MB.

Who should use what

  • You want zero limits and no ads, and enter holdings by hand: Crypton.
  • You want privacy and Apple Watch / Mac: Crypto Pro.
  • You want automatic sync and have 10 assets or fewer: Delta.
  • You hold crypto and US stocks: HODL or Delta.
  • You research coins as much as you track them: CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap.

Use cases

  • A morning glance at the value of your holdings (Crypton, CoinGecko).
  • Keeping an eye on 10 coins or fewer with alerts (HODL, Delta free plans).
  • Tracking wallets and exchanges without paying (Delta, within the limit).
  • Looking up a coin before you buy it (CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap).

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free crypto portfolio tracker for iPhone?

It depends on whether you need syncing. For a free manual list with no limits, Crypton or CoinGecko; for automatic sync, Delta (up to 10 assets); for news and research, CoinMarketCap.

Which free crypto tracker has no asset limit?

Crypton and CoinGecko list no cap on the free tier. Delta and HODL cap the free tier at 10.

Which free tracker connects to exchanges and wallets?

Delta's free plan lists unlimited connections within its 10-asset limit; Crypto Pro lists imports from wallet addresses and exchange API keys. Crypton does not connect to any.

Which crypto tracker has no ads and collects the least data?

Crypton has no ads and its privacy label lists only diagnostics and usage data not linked to you. Crypto Pro says it has no analytics and no ads.
