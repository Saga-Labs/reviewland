About Reviewland and how we check

Reviewland is run by Saga Labs, a small studio that makes iPhone apps (Photo Vault, Drop, Pinku, 3 Tasks, Crypton, Dora, Fast AI and Brainy). Contact: sagalabs@proton.me.

Our apps come first, and we say so

When one of our apps fits a question, it is listed first with a "Our app" label and a disclosure at the top of the page. Competitors are linked to their normal App Store pages and shown with their real data, including where they are better than ours.

What we check

  • Price and in-app purchases, version and release date, rating and number of ratings, languages, minimum iOS: from the App Store listing (US store unless the page says otherwise) on the date shown.
  • Privacy: the App Store privacy label as published by each developer.
  • Features: only what the developer states on its App Store listing or official site. We do not invent reviews or scores.

Updates

We recheck each page about once a month. The "Updated" date only changes when the data has actually been checked again.

Reviewland is run by Saga Labs, which makes some of the apps we rank. We say so on every page where that happens.
