About Reviewland and how we check

Reviewland is made by a small team that also builds iPhone apps.

Our apps are one more option, and we say so

When one of our apps fits a question, it is included as one more option, placed where it fits rather than first, and a single line at the top of the page says it is ours. Competitors are linked to their normal App Store pages and shown with their real data, including where they are better than ours.

What we check

  • Price and in-app purchases, version and release date, rating and number of ratings, languages, minimum iOS: from the App Store listing (US store unless the page says otherwise) on the date shown.
  • Privacy: the App Store privacy label as published by each developer.
  • Features: only what the developer states on its App Store listing or official site. We do not invent reviews or scores.

Updates

We recheck each page about once a month. The "Updated" date only changes when the data has actually been checked again.

Rankings of iPhone apps. Every price, version and privacy label is copied from the App Store on the date shown.
