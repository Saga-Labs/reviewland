Poe alternatives for iPhone

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

Poe puts many companies' models in one app, from $4.99 a month (Basic) to $19.99 (Plus). These five do the same in their own way. For answers with sources, Perplexity, which is free. For the longest model list plus images and video, Monica. To switch model in the middle of a chat, Ask AI. To use your own API keys, OpenCat.

Poe's App Store listing still names older models such as o3, GPT-4.5 and Claude 3.7 Sonnet. Check which models each app lists today, and whether you want custom bots, which only Poe offers here.

1

Perplexity: AI Agent & Search

Best for: answers with sources, using several companies' models behind one search box.

  • Free, with answers backed by sources and citations, per its listing.
  • Pro Search and Deep Research; uses top models from several companies.
  • The most ratings here, about 520,000.

Limits: it is built around search, so you do not compare models side by side as in Poe.

2

ChatOn AI - Chat Bot Assistant

Best for: writing, images and math help, with ready prompts, from several models.

  • Its listing names GPT-5, Gemini 3.5, Claude Sonnet 5 and Sonar.
  • Over 100 ready-made prompts, image creation and photo-to-text.

Limits: mostly weekly plans, and its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You.

App Store page →

3

Monica AI: Ultimate AI Assist

Best for: the longest model list, plus images and video, closest to Poe's mix.

  • Its listing names GPT-5.4 Pro, Claude 4.6 Sonnet and Opus, Gemini 3.1 Pro and image models such as Nano Banana 2.
  • Voice mode, writing, translation and an image and video studio.
  • Its privacy label is only Data Not Linked to You.

Limits: its last update was on 21 July 2026, and the bigger plans cost $199 a year.

App Store page →

4

All AI Models in One: Ask AI

Best for: switching model in the middle of a conversation, as you would between Poe bots.

  • Pick a model, ask, and switch whenever you like in the same chat, per its listing.
  • Reads photos you attach and reads answers aloud; Pro adds every model, web search and a higher limit; a few free messages a day.

Limits: chat only: no images, videos, files or custom bots, and its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You.

App Store page →

5

OpenCat - AI Chat, Agent, MCP

Best for: people who already pay for API keys and want one app for all of them, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  • Its listing says you can use your own API key, its Cloud subscription, local open-source models or Apple Intelligence.
  • Supports MCP and tool calls; chats are stored locally and synced through iCloud.
  • An AI keyboard and system-wide translation with Pro.

Limits: bringing your own keys takes setup, and it has under 1,000 ratings.

Who should use what

  • Answers with sources: Perplexity.
  • Ready prompts for writing: ChatOn.
  • The most models, plus images: Monica.
  • Switching model mid-chat: Ask AI.
  • Your own API keys: OpenCat.

Use cases

  • Comparing two answers to the same question: Ask AI, switching model in the chat, or Monica.
  • Research with links you can check: Perplexity.
  • Using a Claude or Gemini key you already pay for: OpenCat.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. An app is here only if its listing says it gives access to models from several companies; Genie and Chat AI are left out because their listings do not name their models. We did not test the answers.

Order: first the apps with the most users and the clearest free option, then the ones for one way of working. See also DeepSeek alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free alternative to Poe?

Perplexity is a free app that uses several companies' models. Ask AI gives a few free messages a day, and OpenCat works with your own API keys.

Which app lets me compare AI models like Poe?

Ask AI lets you switch model in the same conversation, per its listing; Monica lists the most models in one app.

Is there a Poe alternative with custom bots?

None of these five listings describes building custom bots the way Poe does.

Which is the most private?

Monica and OpenCat have privacy labels with only Data Not Linked to You, and OpenCat stores chats on the device and in your iCloud, per its listing.
