Calculator# alternatives for iPhone

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

Calculator# (by Salman Ashraf) looks like a calculator and hides photos, videos, notes and passwords behind it. It is free with ads and limits, and the full version is $4.99 a year; its privacy label includes data used to track you. For the same trick of a vault inside an ordinary-looking app, Converter: Hidden Photo Vault (a currency converter, paid once). For the best-known vault with a decoy password, Private Photo Vault. For a fake password and a custom icon at a low price, PV - Secret Photo Album. For a calculator-looking icon with no account and nothing uploaded, Photo Vault.

If you only need photos out of your camera roll, iOS also has a locked Hidden album in Photos; see apps to hide photos.

1

Converter: Hidden Photo Vault

Best for: the closest idea to Calculator#: a vault hidden inside an ordinary-looking utility, paid once.

  • Its listing says the vault sits inside a currency converter, and files are encrypted and kept on the phone and in your own iCloud.
  • Sold as a one-time purchase, "without subscription", and its privacy label says Data Not Collected.

Limits: photos and documents only (no notes, passwords or browser like Calculator#), English only, needs iOS 18.2, and its last update was in December 2025.

Private Photo Vault - Pic Safe

Best for: the best-known vault, with a decoy password and a report of who tried to get in.

  • About 1 million ratings, the most of any vault here, and updated on 2 November 2026.
  • Its listing names a decoy password, a face-down lock, a break-in report with a photo and GPS, and a private browser.

Limits: the free version does not back up to its Cloud Vault, per its listing, and its privacy label includes data used to track you.

3

PV - Secret Photo Album

Best for: the most features per dollar: fake password, custom icon and an emergency switch.

  • Its listing names a fake password, a custom app icon, and an Emergency Switch that jumps to another app when the phone faces down.
  • Free unlimited storage on the phone, a trash folder and a photo editor; ads until you pay.

Limits: the fake password needs a paid plan, per its listing, and the privacy label is the widest here.

4

Photo Vault: Hide Pics Video

Best for: a calculator-looking icon with no account, no ads and nothing uploaded unless you choose.

  • Its listing says you can make the icon look like Calculator, Notes or Weather, and flipping the phone face down jumps to Safari, Calendar or Messages.
  • A separate password for each album; no account and no server; privacy label Data Not Collected.
  • Optional end-to-end encrypted copy in your own iCloud, or an encrypted backup file you keep.

Limits: the icon looks like a calculator but the app does not work as one, and the free version is limited (Premium adds unlimited photos and the iCloud copy).

Safe Lock - Hidden Photo Vault

Best for: a decoy gallery and a custom icon with a privacy label of Data Not Collected.

  • Its listing names a fake password that opens a fake gallery, a custom app icon, break-in reports with photos, and locking by shaking or flipping.
  • Photos and videos are saved only on the device, per its listing; 13 languages.

Limits: no automatic cloud copy: you export to a cloud service yourself.

Hide Photos Video -Hide it Pro

  • Its listing says a disguise screen makes the app look as if it crashed when someone opens it.

Limits: English only, and its last update was in January 2026.

Who should use what

  • A vault inside a working-looking utility, paid once: Converter.
  • Decoy password and break-in report: Private Photo Vault.
  • Fake password and custom icon, cheapest yearly plan: PV - Secret Photo Album.
  • Calculator-looking icon, no account, nothing uploaded: Photo Vault.
  • A fake gallery behind a second password: Safe Lock.
  • A fake crash screen: Hide it Pro.

Use cases

  • Someone borrows your phone and asks you to open the app: a decoy password (Private Photo Vault, PV, Safe Lock) shows a different set of photos.
  • You don't want a vault app on your home screen at all: Converter, or Photo Vault with the Calculator icon.
  • You keep passwords and notes in Calculator# too: none of these six does all of it. Private Photo Vault adds a private browser and Safe Lock secret notes; for passwords, Apple's Passwords app.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. An app is here only if its listing names a way to hide or disguise the vault and it was updated in the last 12 months. We did not test how well each disguise holds up.

Order: first the apps whose disguise is closest to Calculator#'s, then the rest. Left out: Calculator Lock - Secure Vault (last update December 2024). See also Private Photo Vault alternatives and photo vaults that collect no data.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Calculator#?

For the same idea of a vault hidden inside an ordinary-looking app, Converter: Hidden Photo Vault. For the biggest, best-known vault, Private Photo Vault. For a calculator-looking icon with no account, Photo Vault.

Is there a calculator vault app without ads?

Photo Vault shows no ads and can use a Calculator icon, but it does not work as a calculator. Converter works the same way with a currency converter and is paid once. Calculator# is free with ads until you pay $4.99 a year.

Which photo vault has a fake password?

Private Photo Vault (decoy password), PV - Secret Photo Album (fake password, paid plan) and Safe Lock (fake password that opens a fake gallery), per their listings.

Which of these collect no data?

Converter, Photo Vault and Safe Lock show Data Not Collected on their App Store privacy labels on 3 November 2026.
