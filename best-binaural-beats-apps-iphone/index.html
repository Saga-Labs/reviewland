Binaural beats apps for iPhone

Updated 4 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 4 November 2026

Binaural beats play a slightly different tone in each ear, so they need headphones. For the biggest library with control over each frequency, Moongate. To pay once with no data collected, BrainWave ($5.99). For a Moongate-style library in 17 languages, Anima. For a few binaural tracks inside a simple sleep and breathing app with no ads, Drop. To set the exact frequency yourself for free, Binaural Beats Generator +.

We list what each app offers, not whether binaural beats work: the health claims on these pages are each developer's own.

1

Moongate: Binaural Beats

Best for: the most complete binaural app, with control over how strong each frequency is.

  • Its listing says every soundscape is checked by audio analysis, and you can adjust the intensity of the active frequencies.
  • Backdrop mode plays under podcasts or audiobooks; timers, favorites and playlists; the most ratings here.

Limits: English only, a subscription for the full library, and its privacy label includes data used to track you.

BrainWave: 40 Binaural Series

Best for: paying once, with no subscription and no data collected.

  • Its listing names multi-stage programs for sleep, focus, memory, energy, power nap and anxiety, plus 20 pure binaural frequencies.
  • Runs alongside Spotify or audiobooks; its notes say it has been sold for 18 years.

Limits: you pay before you can try it, and the listing shows no update since September.

Anima: Binaural Beats

Best for: the same kind of library as Moongate, in 17 languages.

  • Its listing says each soundscape states its target frequency, with adjustable intensity and background play.
  • Sound therapy for tinnitus, per its listing.

Limits: the priciest lifetime plan here, and its privacy label includes data used to track you.

Sleep Sounds & Focus: Drop

Best for: a few binaural tracks inside a calm, ad-free sleep and breathing app, in 33 languages.

  • Version 2.14 added four binaural tracks: delta for sleep, alpha, theta and gamma at 40 Hz, each a tone per ear over soft brown noise.
  • Also 56 nature and noise sounds, short guided breathing, a sleep timer up to 8 hours and no ads.

Limits: only four binaural tracks, all with Drop Pro, no custom frequencies and no mixer; its App Store description does not list them yet, and it has only 12 ratings.

Binaural Beats Generator +

Best for: making your own beat: pick the exact frequency, for free.

  • Its listing says you choose a binaural beat from 1 to 50 Hz and a base frequency from 20 to 1500 Hz, and play to AirPlay devices.
  • From TMSOFT, the makers of the White Noise app.

Limits: just a tone generator: no music, nature sounds or programs, English only, and its label includes data used to track you.

Beatfulness

Best for: the cheapest subscription here, with sessions for sleep, study and meditation.

  • Its listing names sessions for deep sleep, study and work, focus, learning and memory, deep relaxation and a morning boost, plus advanced pure tones.
  • Privacy label Data Not Linked to You.

Limits: many sessions are spiritual (chakras, astral projection), English only, and its developer's notes say updates paused for a long time.

Who should use what

  • Biggest library, adjustable intensity: Moongate.
  • Pay once, no data collected: BrainWave.
  • In your language: Anima (17) or Drop (33).
  • A few tracks in a simple sleep app: Drop.
  • Your own frequency, free: Binaural Beats Generator +.
  • Cheapest subscription: Beatfulness.

Use cases

  • Falling asleep: delta or theta tracks with a sleep timer (Moongate, Anima, Drop).
  • Studying or working: gamma at 40 Hz or beta; Moongate, BrainWave and Anima play under other apps.
  • Only want rain or white noise, not beats: see free sleep sound apps.

How we checked

On 4 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data, and for Drop the app's own sound list. An app is here only if it offers binaural beats and was updated since August 2026. We did not measure the frequencies or test any effect.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best binaural beats app for iPhone?

Moongate has the biggest library and lets you adjust each frequency. BrainWave is $5.99 once with no data collected. Binaural Beats Generator + is free if you only want a tone.

Do I need headphones for binaural beats?

Yes. Each ear gets a slightly different tone, so the listings of these apps say to use headphones or earbuds.

Is there a free binaural beats app?

Binaural Beats Generator + lists no purchases. Moongate, Anima, Drop and Beatfulness are free to download with paid plans; Drop's binaural tracks need Drop Pro.

Which frequency is for sleep and which for focus?

The apps use delta (below 4 Hz) and theta for sleep, alpha for calm, and beta and gamma (40 Hz) for focus, per their listings.
