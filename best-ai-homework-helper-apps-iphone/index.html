Best AI homework helper apps for iPhone

Updated 3 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 3 November 2026

For math, Photomath: its step-by-step explanations are free and its privacy label is the lightest here. For every science subject with a voice tutor, Gauth. For real human tutors, Brainly. For homework that is not math, or to check an answer with a second AI model, a general chat such as Ask AI.

All seven are free to download; the full explanations are usually paid, and several plans are weekly. Use the steps to learn the method, and check what your school allows.

1

Photomath

Best for: math homework you want to understand step by step, for free.

  • Scan a math problem and get the steps, with several methods, word-problem help and interactive graphs, per its listing.
  • Basic step-by-step explanations are free; Plus adds animated tutorials and textbook solutions.
  • The only app here whose privacy label is only Data Not Linked to You, and 32 languages.

Limits: math only, and its last update was on 31 August 2026.

2

Gauth: AI Study Companion

Best for: every science subject, with a voice tutor when you are stuck.

  • Step-by-step explanations across subjects, an AI Live Tutor by voice with a whiteboard, and human experts 24/7, per its listing.
  • Quizzes, flashcards, a writing assistant and a study timer.
  • The most ratings of any homework app here, about 1.5 million.

Limits: the full explanations need Plus, and its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You.

App Store page →

3

Brainly: Your Personal Tutor

Best for: parents who want real human tutors as well as AI.

  • Its listing is written for parents: one-on-one help from human tutors and AI in math, reading, science and more, from kindergarten to grade 12.
  • Scan a math problem for a step-by-step walkthrough.
  • Brainly Tutor adds the live human tutors.

Limits: the live tutors are in the $29.99-a-month plan, and the page lists Plus at several prices, from $2 a month.

App Store page →

4

Answer.AI - Your AI tutor

Best for: high-school students who also want help with AP plans and college applications.

  • Step-by-step explanations, study tools and study groups, per its listing; Pro adds voice chat, AP study plans and scholarship advice.
  • Says it was founded by educators.

Limits: you need an account to chat, it needs iOS 18, and the free tier has ads, per its listing.

App Store page →

5

All AI Models in One: Ask AI

Best for: checking an answer with a second AI model, or homework that is not math.

  • Attach a photo of the question and ask about it, then switch to another model in the same chat to compare, per its listing; models come from several companies, such as Gemini, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity Sonar.
  • Writes, rewrites, summarizes and translates, and reads any answer aloud. Pro adds every model, web search and a much higher limit.

Limits: it is a general chat, not a homework solver: no math steps built for school, no textbook solutions, and it does not read files.

6

Solvely - AI Study Tools

Best for: turning lecture notes, PDFs and videos into quizzes as well as solving problems.

  • Each answer is checked by several AI models, per its listing, and you can photograph a textbook, worksheet or whiteboard.
  • An AI note taker turns YouTube videos, PDFs, slides and recordings into notes, quizzes and flashcards.

Limits: the in-depth explanations are in Unlimited, and the page also sells gems and vouchers.

Quizard AI: Homework Helper

Best for: multiple-choice and short-answer questions before a quiz.

  • Snap a photo of a question, including math, and get the answer with an explanation, per its listing.

Limits: English only, and the page lists many prices for the same plans.

Who should use what

  • Math, free steps: Photomath.
  • All sciences, voice tutor: Gauth.
  • A parent who wants human tutors: Brainly.
  • AP and college prep: Answer.AI.
  • Essays, languages, history, or a second opinion: Ask AI.
  • Notes and quizzes from lectures: Solvely.
  • Multiple-choice practice: Quizard.

Use cases

  • An algebra worksheet: Photomath for the steps, free.
  • A chemistry question you do not understand: Gauth or Solvely, then ask why each step works.
  • An answer that looks wrong: ask the same question to a second model in Ask AI, or check it in Photomath.

How we checked

On 3 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data. Subjects, free use and features come from each listing; we did not install the apps or test how often they are right.

Order: first the apps built for homework whose listing says clearly what is free, then the general chat and the study and quiz tools. "Socratic AI" on the App Store is not Google's old Socratic app, so it is not here. For general chat apps, see AI chat apps for iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free homework helper app for iPhone?

For math, Photomath: its listing says basic step-by-step explanations are free. Brainly is free with limited features, and Ask AI gives a few messages a day. The others are free to download and sell subscriptions.

Which app solves homework from a photo?

All seven accept a photo of the question, per their listings. Photomath is for math only; Gauth, Brainly, Solvely and Quizard cover several subjects, and Ask AI answers questions about any photo you attach.

Is there a homework app with real tutors?

Brainly Tutor ($29.99 a month) adds live human tutors, and Gauth lists human experts 24/7 next to its AI.

Are AI homework answers always right?

No. Check the steps, not only the answer. Solvely says it cross-checks answers with several models; in Ask AI you can ask a second model yourself.
