Best iPhone apps to track WLFI, TRUMP and USD1 prices (Nov 2026)

Updated 8 November 2026 · Data checked on the App Store on 8 November 2026

To follow WLFI, TRUMP and USD1 with price alerts, CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap and CoinStats all list the three tokens; CoinStats also connects your wallet and has Lock Screen widgets.

For a free Home Screen widget with no account and nothing to buy, Crypton finds all three by search but has no alerts, and Delta suits a portfolio that mixes crypto with stocks.

What happened (8 November 2026)

After the 3 November midterms, Democrats won a House majority according to NBC News and MS NOW projections, and plan to investigate the Trump family's crypto businesses, including World Liberty Financial (the WLFI token and the USD1 stablecoin) and the TRUMP meme coin, crypto.news reported; investigations could begin as early as February 2027. The White House rejected the plans; before the vote a spokeswoman had called Democrats' agenda a "witch hunt", per the same report. CNBC wrote that the result puts the crypto industry on the defensive in Washington, and on 8 November the Financial Times ran the story on its front page under the headline "Crypto industry braces for reckoning over Trump ties".

1

CoinGecko: Crypto Tracker

Best for: following WLFI, TRUMP and USD1 from the same source as the price table above, with alerts.

  • CoinGecko's own data lists Official Trump, World Liberty Financial and USD1, so you can add all three to a watchlist.
  • Its listing offers personalised price alerts and large market movement alerts for watchlist coins, a portfolio with profit and loss, and a Home Screen widget.
  • Tracks categories such as meme coins, which is where TRUMP sits.

Limits: English only, and the listing does not say how many alerts are free.

CoinMarketCap: Crypto Tracker

Best for: the most-rated tracker, with alerts on any of the three tokens.

  • CoinMarketCap has pages for Official Trump, World Liberty Financial and World Liberty Financial USD (USD1).
  • Customisable price alerts and watchlists; tracks 45,000+ active markets, per its listing.
  • About 173,000 US ratings, the most here, and 21 languages.

Limits: its listing says to sign up to make a portfolio (incognito mode keeps data on the phone), it does not mention a widget, and its plans are the most expensive here.

CoinStats: Crypto Portfolio

Best for: holding WLFI or TRUMP in a wallet or exchange and wanting it tracked automatically, with alerts and Lock Screen widgets.

  • coinstats.app has pages for Official Trump, World Liberty Financial and USD1.
  • Connects 300+ wallets and exchanges and shows real-time profit and loss; prices for 20,000+ coins, per its listing.
  • Price, volume or market-cap alerts on any coin, and widgets on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch.

Limits: many overlapping paid plans, and the full set of insights needs Premium.

Crypto Tracker: Crypton

Best for: a free watchlist of WLFI, TRUMP and USD1 on the Home Screen, with no account and nothing to pay.

  • Live prices for the top 250 coins, plus search for any coin listed on CoinGecko, so WLFI, TRUMP and USD1 can go into Favorites, per its listing.
  • Portfolio with profit and loss on every coin, and Home Screen widgets for prices and for your portfolio.
  • No in-app purchases; English only, iOS 18 or later.

Limits: no price alerts in the live version, no Lock Screen widget or Apple Watch app, and no wallet or exchange sync; fewest ratings here (186). For alerts, see the crypto price alert apps.

Delta by eToro

Best for: tracking crypto and stocks in one portfolio, with alerts.

  • Tracks crypto, stocks, ETFs, commodities and forex together; 10,000+ coins, per its listing.
  • Smart price alerts for target prices and significant moves, and custom notifications for portfolio milestones.
  • Free plan tracks up to 10 assets; PRO up to 40; PRO+ unlimited.

Limits: we could not confirm on Delta's site that it lists WLFI, TRUMP and USD1, the free plan stops at 10 assets, and its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You.

Who should use what

  • Alerts when TRUMP or WLFI moves sharply: CoinGecko (large-mover alerts) or CoinStats (Auto Alerts).
  • The most-used tracker: CoinMarketCap.
  • Tokens held in a wallet or on an exchange: CoinStats.
  • Just a glance at the three prices, no account: Crypton.
  • Crypto and stocks in one place: Delta.

Use cases

  • See whether USD1 stays at $1: any app that lists it; CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap show its market cap too.
  • Get a notification if TRUMP falls another 10%: CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, CoinStats or Delta (alerts); not Crypton.
  • Keep WLFI and TRUMP on the Home Screen: CoinGecko, CoinStats or Crypton. More in our crypto widget apps and crypto price alert apps rankings.
  • Track holdings without linking a wallet: see the trackers that need no wallet or exchange.

How we checked

On 8 November 2026 we read each app's US App Store page and Apple's lookup data (price, in-app purchases, version, rating and listing text). Whether an app lists WLFI, TRUMP and USD1 was checked on CoinGecko's data, on CoinMarketCap's and CoinStats' coin pages, and in Crypton's listing (it searches CoinGecko); Delta's site did not let us check. We did not install the apps or test how fast alerts arrive.

The news context is attributed to the outlets linked above; this page takes no position on the investigations. None of these apps is an exchange, and nothing here is financial advice.

Frequently asked questions

Which app shows the WLFI price on iPhone?

CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap and CoinStats list World Liberty Financial (WLFI), and Crypton finds it by search because it uses CoinGecko data.

Can I get an alert for the TRUMP meme coin price?

Yes, with CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, CoinStats or Delta, which list price alerts. Crypton has no price alerts.

Is USD1 still worth one dollar?

On CoinGecko it traded at about $0.998 from 1 to 8 November 2026, with a market cap of about $3.44 billion.

Why did TRUMP and WLFI fall in November 2026?

On CoinGecko, TRUMP fell about 32% and WLFI about 8% from 1 to 8 November, the week Democrats who won the House said they plan to investigate the Trump family's crypto businesses, according to crypto.news and CNBC. This page does not attribute any price move to a cause, and it is not financial advice.

Do I need an account to track these tokens?

Not in Crypton (the account only syncs). CoinMarketCap needs one for its portfolio but has an incognito mode; the other listings do not say one is required.
